My favourite subject

Maths

Numbers can do some wonderfully surprising things. Here are a few facts I think are fascinating.

01

Almost 1 is exactly 1

The decimal 0.999... (with the 9s continuing forever) is equal to 1. Since 1/3 is 0.333... , multiplying both by 3 gives this surprising result.

02

A googol is enormous

A googol is 1 followed by 100 zeros. A googolplex is 1 followed by a googol zeros, so there are far too many digits to write out in the observable universe.

03

Pi never settles down

Pi's decimal digits go on forever without repeating in a fixed pattern. We use the symbol π for the ratio between a circle's circumference and its diameter.

04

The 1089 number trick

Choose a three-digit number whose first and last digits differ by at least 2, reverse it, then subtract the smaller from the larger. Reverse that answer and add the two results: you get 1089.

05

Honeycomb geometry

Regular hexagons fit together with no gaps, which makes them a clever shape for covering a surface efficiently.
