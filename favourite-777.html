My favourite number

What makes 777 special?

It is a cool-looking number with some neat maths behind it, plus a few popular symbolic meanings.

777
01

A factor surprise

777 = 3 × 7 × 37. Its digits also add up to 21, so 777 is divisible by 3.

02

Three sevens together

777 is a repeated-digit number, sometimes called a repdigit. In base ten, it is seven times 111.

03

A popular symbol

Some numerology and “angel number” traditions associate 777 with luck, reflection, or spiritual growth. These are cultural beliefs, not scientific facts, and people interpret the number in different ways.

04

Seen in games

Three sevens are often used as a jackpot symbol in slot-machine imagery, which helped make 777 widely associated with good luck.

Maths note: The factors and digit sum can be checked with arithmetic. Symbolic meanings are traditions and stories, not properties of the number itself.
